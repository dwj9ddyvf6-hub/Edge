# Validation and use

## Completed checks

The updated skill passed its structure validator. Cross-reference and file checks were performed across the original and added material. Independent mathematical and operational review led to explicit hard-fault exits, atomic revalidation before reservation, point-in-time regime fitting, comparable continuation rights in information-value calculations, and clearer no-trade notation.

The dependency-free offline arbiter passed 20 synthetic test methods, including paired robust value versus incorrectly subtracted minima; strict hurdles; deterministic selection; action-specific freshness; expiry; evidence and approval flags; unknown or stale own state; infeasible incumbent recovery; opposing pending orders; simultaneous reservations; joint portfolio stress; signed limits; malformed probabilities/units; partial fills; finite computation caps; and numeric validation. An oversized-integer input was added to the malformed-number test after review. These are invariant checks, not financial backtests.

Two fresh-context applications used only the skill and raw fictional desk problems. One diagnosed unsupported routing uplift, future-fitted regime parameters, invalid selected/reset monitoring, size effects and delayed evidence, then proposed identifiable experiments. The other computed the paired robust difference correctly, rejected unsafe flatten/retry logic, retained pending exposure and removed a two-second optional computation from a 100-millisecond quote path. They produced substantive solutions without being given the intended diagnosis. These are two qualitative cases, not a benchmark or proof of universal LLM behavior.

Checked illustrative arithmetic includes candidate utilities (5,1) versus incumbent (0,4): separate minima differ by +1, while worst paired improvement is -3; and the probability-interval examples producing worst mean improvements 1.95 and 2.35. A possible long inventory of 40–100 followed by an 80-unit sale can become 40 short. No live observations were used to manufacture these examples.

## Not established

No strategy backtest on new market data, forward paper-trading record, actual queue-fill experiment, live trade, production adapter, causal uplift estimate or real trading latency benchmark was performed in this addendum. No return multiple or orders-of-magnitude improvement is claimed. Source findings and engineering synthesis are kept distinct. The source register states which paper sections were inspected and which entries had abstract-only access.

The offline kernel consumes supplied models; it does not estimate their correctness. It supports additive scenario constraints, not arbitrary nonlinear margin. It does not implement durable journaling, atomic external reservations, broker deduplication or wall-clock deadlines. The runtime chapter specifies those integration requirements and tests.
