# The potential-edge census: grade a whole estate by potential, not by proof

This reference is the grading instrument for the moment after a hunt, when an estate holds dozens or hundreds of
candidates and no forward window has had time to decide any of them. It was built and run on 15 November 2026
over one desk's full production of the preceding days: every hunt wave, factory family, chaos lane and candidate
queue inventoried, the top of the estate scored in full, and a standing register of 198 objects published (2
exam-passed, 18 live candidates, 66 leads, 59 watch, 22 avoidance, 31 resolved-against).

Read this file when a research program must rank what it found before proof is possible, when a hunt is being
reported, and whenever someone proposes grading a hunt on proven trades. Use it with
[edge-find-methods.md](edge-find-methods.md) (how the finds were made) and
[consensus-lens.md](consensus-lens.md) (the audit each object must also face).

## The measure

**Success is potential edge found and armed, not proven trades.** Proof requires a forward window and a time
machine; nobody honest can produce it on the day of the hunt. The hunt's product is candidates whose evidence
survives honest deflation at their true trial count, whose mechanism is named, whose falsifier is registered,
and whose forward accrual is wired so the window can decide. Grading a hunt on proven trades misreports good
work as empty: this census's predecessor, run on the proof measure, reported itself near-empty while sitting on
its largest find.

## The rubric

Score every candidate on five fields, taken ONLY from numbers printed in its own source file. Nothing enters the
arithmetic from opinion, reputation or a model's say-so.

- **E, deflated evidence (0-3).** 0: no numbers. 1: external or vendor numbers only, or own numbers that fail
  their own printed deflation. 2: own in-sample numbers with deflation printed and survived at lead strength.
  3: own in-sample exam passed at the true trial count (DSR at effective N, SPA, PBO printed).
- **M, mechanism (0-2).** 0: none claimed. 1: named. 2: named and specific: who pays, why, and through what pipe.
- **D, consensus distance (0-2).** 0: the consensus trade restated. 1: a named baseline with a plausible
  difference source. 2: a difference conditioned on private data only this estate has.
- **A, accrual (0-2).** 0: no forward route. 1: logging or a dated one-shot grade. 2: a live forward falsifier
  running, or armed with a registered rule.
- **S, speed (0-2).** 0: first forward read beyond six months. 1: months. 2: days to weeks.

Total /11. Rank by total; ties break by E, then by D. Model seats may read the candidates (see
[typed-lens-panels.md](typed-lens-panels.md)); seat output never enters the arithmetic.

## Procedure

1. **Inventory everything, counted.** List every source population with its object count and status before
   scoring anything: waves, factories, lanes, queues, registers. The inventory is auditable or the census is a
   story.
2. **Score in full the top of the estate.** Each fully scored candidate prints its five fields, the evidence
   behind E in its own numbers, its falsifier, its accrual route and its next measurement date. The rest are
   printed in tier tables with the one number that matters and the reason they are not top tier.
3. **Print failures at full size.** A candidate that fails deflation is not hidden and not killed: it is printed
   as a lead or a resolved negative, with its numbers. A resolved first read against a construction stays on the
   board beside the named open question that keeps the object alive, if one exists.
4. **Publish the register.** Every object lands in exactly one standing class (exam-passed, live candidate, lead,
   watch, avoidance, resolved-against) with its score and its next step. The register is the census's durable
   product; the prose is commentary on it.
5. **Reexamine item by item.** No object is classified away by category. Every decided-against object keeps its
   named future routes (a different expression, a basket form, a conditioner use, a variation with its own
   falsifier) printed beside it, and a later pass works the objects one by one. A summary that converts the
   objects nearest to tradeable and waves at the rest has not run a census; it has run a triage and mislabeled
   it.

## Checks

- If a score cannot be traced to a number printed in the object's own source file, the score is wrong; fix the
  score, not the file.
- Counts at every stage: generated, scored, surviving, registered. Reduce combination cells to the distinct
  objects they describe before counting anything as a find.
- A candidate's falsifier and next measurement are part of its score's meaning: an A0 object with a high total
  is a photograph, not a program.

## Evidence

In the founding run, the top tier held twelve fully scored objects and the board separated cleanly: the flagship
overnight premium scored at the top on exam-passed evidence with its falsifier already accruing, while a
celebrated expression candidate scored 8 with its first resolver read resolved AGAINST its current construction
(20 of 21 fires stopped at -1R on the original basis) and stayed on the board only because a named measurement
question (the resolution basis, not the idea) remained open and testable. Two second-tier objects printed their
own demotions: a 90.2 percent hit rate at n 61 whose BH q of 0.188 failed deflation, and an oversold complex whose
q near 0.7 made it indistinguishable from the best of thousands of nulls. The census did not protect the estate's
favorites; that is what it is for.
