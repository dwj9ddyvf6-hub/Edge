# Edge-find methods: how the finds were actually made

This reference is the operating playbook for hunting edge in records an estate already holds. It distils a two-day alpha hunt (14 and 15 November 2026) in which 25 finds were extracted from one desk's own records, every find autopsied in the same pass that reported it. The source record is the desk's edge-find method ledger: 25 autopsy entries, a geology table, a vein map, a methods ranking and a negative ledger. Only aggregate statistics appear here; no raw vendor data, accounts or personal identifiers.

Read this file when a hunt is being planned or run: when a research program must decide where to dig next, when a lane reports a null and someone must decide whether the null is a verdict or a route failure, when a find needs its forward check designed, or when a reported find needs its method audited. The governing pattern fits on one page. The nine methods follow as operational entries. The autopsy protocol, the vein map, the anti-patterns and the cold-start checklist close the file.

## The governing pattern

**No find in the hunt came from a new dataset bought or a new model asked. Every find came from looking at records the estate already held through a structure it had not applied to them: a decomposition, a higher order, a longer span, a lower layer, an exact definition, a primary file, a rebuilt instrument.**

Three sentences carry the whole pattern:

1. **The soil is almost never new.** The richest ground was the estate's own records one layer below where its printed verdicts sat: beneath the reports, inside the producers' own code, in the identifier grammar of its rejection logs, in working files a digest had summarized wrongly. Before buying data or asking a model, inventory what is already held at the layer below the layer you have been reading.
2. **The tool is a structure, never a search.** Nine moves produced all 25 finds. They are ranked below by yield, and the ranking is the next hunt's checklist: apply the top moves first to the next unlooked area.
3. **The conditions make finds believable.** The same work run without the believability conditions produces noise dressed as nuggets. The conditions are not optional garnish; they are what separates a find from a story.

### The believability conditions

Every test in the hunt ran under all of these. A result produced without one is labelled with the missing condition, never silently upgraded.

- **A falsifier is registered before the run.** Every candidate carries, in advance, the result that retires it: a count of forward observations and the line they must cross. Lane 2 of the hunt registered that two consecutive barren mutation campaigns would demote the mutation premise as a historical screen. When the second campaign printed zero survivors, the premise was demoted in the same pass that reported the campaign, with no plea for a third run on the same ground. A falsifier written after the result is a rationalization, not a falsifier.
- **Deflation happens at the true trial count.** A rebuilt or rediscovered object is charged to the family it belongs to, not allowed to pose as a fresh discovery. The lawful FIT rebuild was charged at family count 46, not at 1. A separator census is deflated inside its own cohort and order family (Benjamini-Hochberg), and a selected strategy is deflated for selection (deflated Sharpe ratio, probability of backtest overfitting) at the count of everything actually tried.
- **Statistics are clustered by the independent unit.** Symbol-sessions are not independent when the unit is the night; fills are not independent when the unit is the entry date. The midday segment read minus 28.9 basis points pooled over 294 symbol-sessions and did not survive session clustering (clustered t minus 1.53); it was demoted in print from find to registered hypothesis. Print the cluster count beside every cell, and refuse verdict language where clusters are 1 to 3.
- **Counts are honest at every stage.** Print the denominator at every stage of a funnel: candidates generated, scored, surviving. Reduce combination cells to the distinct objects they describe before counting finds: one hunt enumerated 186,180 cells, tested 56,512, and reduced 4,111 verdict cells to the 737 distinct sleeve sets they actually described. Counting cells as discoveries is claim compression wearing a lab coat.
- **The hunt is graded on potential edge found and armed, never on proven trades.** Proof requires a forward window nobody has yet. Grading a hunt on proven trades misreports good work as empty: the hunt's first census, run on the proof measure, reported itself near-empty while sitting on its largest find. A find is graded by the strength of its retrospective evidence and by whether its forward check is wired, dated or named, in that order of preference.
- **Kills come only from the estate's own resolved samples hitting pre-registered falsifiers.** External literature designs the test; it is never the verdict. A proxy disagreement is not a kill. A conditioner read while its phenomenon is switched off can produce only a forward check, never a find and never a corpse.

## Method 1: Decomposition

**What it is.** Split an aggregate into its natural parts and grade each part alone, on statistics clustered by the independent unit. The aggregate is a bundle, not a fact; the edge, if one exists, lives in a part, and the part determines the tradeable form.

**Trigger.** Reach for decomposition whenever the object in front of you is an aggregate that has only ever been graded whole: a basket's close-to-close return, a day treated as one observation, a fleet's or a book's pooled realized returns, a family's pooled phase curve. The observable sign is a flat or mediocre aggregate sitting on top of parts nobody has priced separately.

**Procedure.**
1. Cut the aggregate at its own joints, not at round boundaries. A trading day cuts into overnight, open drive, midday and close hour because that is the session's own structure. Realized returns cut by hold phase (night 1, nights 2 to 3, nights 4 to 5, night 4 and beyond) and by family.
2. Compute every part on the same rows, so the parts are comparable and sum to the whole.
3. Audit the population for basis contaminants before believing any part: instruments whose price dynamics are not the signal's (option-contract fills inside an equity population, benchmark self-trades inside an excess-return population). A contaminant class gets its own labelled rows in the register, never a silent exclusion and never a place inside the headline aggregate.
4. Grade each part alone, clustered by the independent unit. Whatever survives direction but not clustering is registered as a forward hypothesis with a named falsifier, neither promoted nor buried.
5. For the surviving part, run the cost audit as a separate question at the true execution prints. What a part earns and what it costs to touch are two measurements, not one.

**Worked example (ledger entry 1, the overnight Focus-names edge).** The Focus basket's close-to-close return decomposed into legs. The intraday leg was worthless (Sharpe 0.01); the overnight leg carried everything: plus 16.46 basis points a night over 2,229 sessions back to 2018, t 4.97, all 29 names positive, plus 12.24 basis points at t 5.24 with every gap night removed, against SPY at plus 3.53 and QQQ at plus 4.85 on the same nights. Discovery exam: deflated Sharpe ratio 0.99994, SPA 0.001 against SPY and 0.002 against QQQ, probability of backtest overfitting 0.067, gross Sharpe 1.67. The cost audit then decided the tradeable form: crossing quoted spreads costs about 27 basis points a night (46.076 basis points measured on the audit night), while closing-auction entry and opening-auction exit cost about 5.8 basis points on the five carry names, against a 15 basis point death line. The bar's close proved to be the closing auction print (exact in 60 of 60 name-sessions); the bar's open is not the opening print (exact in only 4 of 60, one name off by plus 28.765 basis points on average), so any earlier cost read taken off bar opens was mismeasured by up to that much. Forward check: a 60-session falsifier on auction prints, demotion if the mean net of 5 basis points is not positive.

**Second worked example (ledger entry 13, the fleet's phase shape).** The fleet's recorded entry events, priced forward (6,664 of 6,708 priceable at night 1), earn night 1 (plus 43.4 basis points excess against QQQ, n 5,648, win rate 0.569) and bleed in every later phase (nights 2 to 3 minus 152.3, nights 4 to 5 minus 260.5, night 4 and beyond minus 839.1): the recorded fleet is a night-1 book carrying multi-night holds. Phase is a family property, not a fleet property: one family runs the opposite shape (night 1 minus 429.8, nights 2 to 3 plus 1,378.2 at win rate 0.898). The contaminant audit mattered at full size: 1,016 of the 6,664 priceable events were option-contract fills whose phase swings of plus or minus 4,000 to 5,800 basis points are contract geometry, not signal; left inside, they would have swamped every equity cell by an order of magnitude.

**Failure mode.** Decomposition describes; it does not design. A phase decomposition's implied design rule is a hypothesis until tested as a rule on a different population (see Method 5 and the anti-patterns: the night-1-exit transfer failed on the deepest sample in its run). Decomposition also produces nothing when the parts are computed on different rows or different filters and then compared as if they were one object; replicate under a second completeness filter and print both reads with the filter difference named.

**Geology signature.** Location: stored bar records and live dispatcher records. Depth: the raw-row layer. Soil: daily bars over the longest span held, 1-minute bars at finer grain, dispatcher entry events. Cluster: Vein 1 (the overnight vein) for price series; Vein 3 (the exit-and-phase vein) for realized returns.

## Method 2: Second-route persistence

**What it is.** A failed route is rerouted, never accepted as a verdict. Route failure is not task impossibility: one route failing proves that route failed. Preserve the exact error, revise the hypothesis about where the thing lives, and take the highest-confidence alternative route. If the same class of action fails twice without a revised hypothesis, stop and reassess; with a revised hypothesis, keep going.

**Trigger.** Reach for it whenever a result is about to be reported as absent, impossible, blocked or unmeasurable, and the evidence is a failed lookup, a failed join, a missing file or a dead feed. The observable sign is a verdict whose entire support is a route: "not in the cache", "the endpoints returned nothing", "the other party has not replied".

**Procedure.**
1. Name the block precisely: which items, what count, which sources were actually searched, with each failure preserved exactly (the status code, the timeout, the empty reply).
2. Ask where the producing system itself gets the thing. For a market series, read the consumer's own code for its live provenance: venue, pair, interval. The feed production serves is the first route for its own history, and provenance-matched data outranks any substitute series.
3. Try routes in confidence order, printing each failure. Two failed routes that share one missing input are one failure, not two.
4. When the input lands, prove the plumbing from inside the results (a split that can only occur if the input was delivered correctly), and name what still could not be sourced instead of fabricating it.
5. Bound what remains missing arithmetically before naming any external gate: counted total minus records in hand equals the true remainder (this step is Method 9's population form, and the two methods compound).

**Worked example (ledger entry 14, the ENJ class).** Twenty-two sleeves stood graded EXECUTION-IMPOSSIBLE because two replay routes had failed for one shared reason: no ENJ bars in any cache on the box. The desk's own code serves Kraken spot ENJ/USD live, so the public route was Kraken's own OHLC API, which landed on the first call: 720 complete 4-hour bars, 2026-07-18 to 2026-11-15 (the venue's 720-candle cap bounds the tape at 120 days, printed as the span limit). The second input, funding history, took three routes with each failure printed (one venue HTTP 451, one HTTP 403) before landing: 584 observations with coverage starting before the first evaluation bar. All 22 sleeves then carried replay grades (2 scored, 8 indicative, 12 no-fire, 0 impossible), every sleeve running 420 clean evaluations. The class's lead stream is direction-certified: 11 entries on 10 dates, forward-10 excess plus 558.3 basis points on 6 priceable entries at t 1.81, inversion minus 392.8. The negative space was measured, not assumed: the 10 funding-squeeze sleeves fired zero times because their entry conjunction occurred 0 times in 420 bars (funding negative on 74 bars, breakouts on 20, zero overlap), hand-verified against both engines' entry code. What could not be sourced was named, not fabricated: historical book depth for one sleeve exists on no public route, and no book was served.

**Second worked example (ledger entry 23, the cache widening).** A forward check's own variable was capped at 2 of 5 names because three names had no 1-minute file at all and the cache's intersection with the study universe was 6 of 30 names. The widening was run as the work, the same evening the cap was named: bars for 29 of 30 names over the full 53-session stored window, 1,033,782 bars added, coverage on the check's names rising from 2 of 5 to 5 of 5 at full depth. Basis was proven before merging (fetched rows checked against cached rows on overlap; the differences characterized as a settlement-timing artifact), every one of the 74,736 pre-existing rows preserved verbatim, and coverage recomputed with the consumer's own function (replica identical on 34 of 34 checks) before any coverage number was printed. A coverage number quoted from a capped cache is a claim about the cache, not about the phenomenon.

**Failure mode.** Persistence without a revised hypothesis is spiralling: repeating the same class of action and calling the repetition diligence. Persistence also fails when it quietly lowers the standard of what lands (an inferred series substituted for the real one without a label). Every landed substitute carries its basis on its face.

**Geology signature.** Location: the consumer's own code first, then the venue or file the code names. Depth: the code layer for the route, the data layer for what lands. Soil: live producer configuration, public venue APIs, rotated logs. Cluster: Vein 4 (the under-the-report vein).

## Method 3: Order-up regeneration

**What it is.** When an exhaustive sweep at order k finds nothing, regenerate the reads at order k plus 1 from the raw rows before concluding there is nothing there. A null at one order is a statement about that order. Joint states express shapes (non-monotonicity, regime containment) that no lower-order read can state.

**Trigger.** Reach for it when a pair space (or single space) has been swept exhaustively and printed zero separators, and the raw rows the sweep was computed from still exist. The observable sign is an exhaustive null standing as a program's verdict, or a higher-order read declared unrecoverable because a curated selection was never persisted.

**Procedure.**
1. Accept the exhaustive lower-order null as a map of where the object is not. Do not re-run it.
2. Regenerate the higher-order reads from the raw rows. Never try to recover a lost selection; recompute the screening function.
3. Fix the grading rules before running: cohorts never mix; deflation families are the cohort's same-order cells; a separator additionally requires its cluster condition (for night-level objects, a night-clustered t of at least 3 over at least 20 nights) and a minimum n.
4. Grade joint states against their own subset base, not the global base.
5. Reduce the separator census mechanically: test every higher-order separator for containing a separating lower-order cell, and report the irreducible set, not the raw count.
6. Before ordering up an enlarged population, complete the semantic namespace: if the same role carries different names across objects, the hunt's ceiling is the namespace, not the statistics. Publish carrier counts per role first. Grade absence as a class of its own: a parameter an object does not carry is a design decision the object made, and the strongest higher-order structure may live in the absence cells.
7. Confirm each surviving pole on a second outcome, and split any population-level bloc inside its families before calling it a mechanism; a bloc that separates only between families is composition, not mechanism.

**Worked example (ledger entry 3, the triple-state separation).** The pair space had been swept exhaustively: zero separators. The curated triple list existed in no persisted file and had been declared unrecoverable. The triples were regenerated from 385 retrospective rows: 114 triples recomputed over 11 usable axes, 189 joint states at n of 10 or more graded, 49 states clearing a 15 percentage point absolute-excess bar over their subset base. The headline cell: bullish posture with RSI overbought (over 70) and both moving-average stacks bull-aligned resolves at a hit rate of 1.00 (n 12, plus 34.1 percentage points over the subset base, z 2.49); the same state without one condition at 0.95 (n 20, plus 29.1 points, z 2.74); the mirror state, bullish with RSI in the 55 to 70 band and bull stacks, at only 0.385 to 0.40 (about minus 26 points). The separation is the non-monotonicity, overbought separating from merely-high inside identical bull posture, which no pair can express. All in-sample, small-n, printed as leads with a forward grade registered.

**Second worked example (ledger entries 12 and 16, the constants hunt and its namespace completion).** First run: 8,054 combination cells enumerated over 21 graded sleeves' extracted constants, 716 tested, 52 lead cells reducing to exactly 3 distinct objects. The Fires cell: 7 sleeves at mean forward-5 excess plus 828.4 basis points against minus 183.8 for the other 14 (difference plus 1,012.2, permutation p 0.0005, BH q 0.0085), confirmed on raw returns (plus 337.2 against minus 242.4, q 0.0255), so it is not a benchmark artifact; its forward-1 confirmation read does not confirm (minus 37.6 against plus 0.9), so the object is a forward-5-horizon object or it is nothing. The strongest higher-order cells were absence classes crossed with that family. Underneath: the estate had no shared parameter namespace, carrier counts per semantic role running from 3 to 21 across the population, so most constants could not form cross-object classes at all. The namespace was then built (31 semantic roles over 10,447 constants) and the identical hunt ran over the enlarged outcome population (104 sleeves carrying the outcome, drawn from 315 graded on some basis, the other 211 counted rather than dropped): 186,180 cells enumerated, 56,512 tested, the Fires pole widened to 9 sleeves at plus 1,330.4 basis points (q 0.0105, raw-confirmed at plus 1,201.2, q 0.0251), one new axis surfaced and deflated in the same pass to mostly between-family composition, and 4,111 verdict cells reduced to 737 distinct objects.

**Failure mode.** Order-up multiplies the trial count; run without same-order deflation families and the irreducible-set reduction, it manufactures discoveries out of nested re-descriptions of one object. It also produces nothing when the raw rows no longer exist and regeneration would mean invention: then the forward accrual is the route, and the register says so.

**Geology signature.** Location: retrospective extraction files, extracted constant maps. Depth: the raw-row layer for regenerated reads; the code layer for constants. Soil: a few hundred retrospective rows over a dozen axes; ten thousand constants over hundreds of objects. Clusters: Vein 5 (the joint-state vein) and Vein 6 (the constants vein).

## Method 4: Maximum-span conditioning

**What it is.** A conditioner found on a short window is a hypothesis about recency until it is re-run on the longest span the data allows, on disjoint subsets of the object it conditions, and segmented at break dates fixed from outside the data. The longest available span is the cheapest rigor there is.

**Trigger.** Reach for it whenever a conditioner, gate or state variable was discovered on a recent window, a single basket, or a pooled span nobody has segmented. The observable signs: a conditioner whose evidence window is the window that suggested it; a pooled multi-year claim whose era structure has never been read; a phenomenon and its conditioner that have only ever been measured together on the same population.

**Procedure.**
1. Take the short-window conditioner as a stated hypothesis, with its sign convention fixed in advance.
2. Rebuild it on the maximum span (every leg the records allow), and print the unconditioned object beside the conditioned split, so the conditioner's contribution is measured, not assumed.
3. Split the object into disjoint subsets (the carrying subset against the rest) and require the conditioner to appear in both; that rules out a subset artifact.
4. Deflate inside the family the conditioner belongs to.
5. Segment the span at externally fixed break dates (dates the literature or the market's own history fixed before the rerun, never dates read off the data's shape). Test each leg in each era against three benchmarks: zero, the global unconditional mean, and its own era's unconditional mean. Read each leg's character beside its mean (median, win rate), which separates a broad-based leg from a tail-carried one.
6. Re-register the claim narrowed to the era and population it actually lives in. Evidence from outside that era is retired, not banked.
7. When widening to new ground, grade the phenomenon and its conditioner as separate objects. A gate can be portable when its premium is not; register the two results as different objects with different forward checks.

**Worked example (ledger entries 6 and 10, the weekend conditioner and its era anatomy).** A conditioner found on recent Mondays (weekend legs sorting on the weekend crypto move) was rebuilt on all 462 weekend legs from 2018 to 2026: after up weekends plus 42.06 basis points (t 4.36, n 250, win rate 0.632), after down weekends minus 20.24 (n 212), a spread of 62.3 basis points against unconditioned weekend legs of only plus 13.47 (t 1.64). It appeared in both halves of its basket (carrying subset plus 47.37 at t 4.03, the rest plus 41.03 at t 4.31, both subsets negative after down weekends) and survived deflation inside its 21-trial family. Then the span was segmented at the literature's own break dates (May 2022; January 2024). Before the first break (225 legs) the conditioner does not exist: up legs plus 10.36, down legs plus 2.80 with the wrong sign against the unconditional mean, spread 7.56 basis points at Welch t 0.32. After it (237 legs) the spread is 114.46 basis points at Welch t 4.96. In the final era the up leg is the strongest single cell in the rerun (plus 98.26, t against zero 5.02, median plus 80.25, win rate 0.738) while the down leg's mean is tail-carried (mean minus 50.13, median plus 8.51). The object was re-registered as a post-May-2022 regime object, the 225 earlier legs retired as supporting evidence, and the forward tracker amended to grade the legs separately against the running unconditional mean. A published asymmetry from the literature (a real down leg, a null up leg) did not hold on this basket; the dating agreed twice over.

**Second worked example (ledger entry 17, the gate that travels without its premium).** On a long-span basket of 18 non-study names (514 nights) the premium itself does not exist (plus 3.74 basis points overnight at t 1.08; across 116 symbols only 3 reach t of 2 and none survives deflation). The same basket's 107 weekend legs split on the weekend crypto move exactly as the study basket's do: up plus 23.24 (t 2.58 against zero, t 2.16 against its own unconditional weekend mean of plus 3.78), down minus 17.57, spread plus 40.81 at Welch t 2.24, widening to plus 49.52 (Welch t 2.83) on the full widened basket. The limit rides on the face: the widened span sits entirely inside the conditioner's final era, so this is a within-era confirmation on new names, not an era test. The conditioner is a market-wide transmission; the premium is a local property of one complex. One question ("does it still work over there?") would have closed the file at the premium's null and lost the gate.

**Failure mode.** Segmentation at breaks chosen from the data's own shape is curve-fitting with extra steps; the breaks must be fixed from outside. Maximum span also cannot rescue a conditioner whose phenomenon is switched off in the span available: a read taken inside the phenomenon's known cold window produces only a forward check (see the anti-patterns).

**Geology signature.** Location: the stacking files and bar caches the conditioner was found on, widened. Depth: the raw-row layer. Soil: hundreds of legs over the full span, an exogenous state series (a weekend crypto move, a prior intraday sign), literature break dates. Cluster: Vein 1 (the overnight vein).

## Method 5: Operator-as-diagnostic

**What it is.** Run a mutation or variation operator as a paired measurement on the object's own stream, not to generate candidates but to expose what the operator was hiding: which gate causes a family's silence, whether a registered parameter is priced for the stream it governs, whether a descriptive structure survives being used as a rule. Every delta is paired against the object's own base, trade by trade, so the comparison is within-object, not between populations.

**Trigger.** Reach for it when a whole family is silent, when a parameter's value was inherited rather than measured, or when a lane's descriptive output implies a design rule. The observable signs: a family-wide zero; a registered constant nobody has varied; a sentence of the form "the shape suggests we should exit (or enter, or size) like this".

**Procedure.**
1. Assemble the population and label every base honestly (registered rule versus convention default), so a variation measured against a convention is never read as one measured against a registered rule. Print the counts of each.
2. State any prediction the prior description implies, family by family, in a table, before the deltas are read.
3. Run the variation set paired per trade: drop the gate, widen it, extend the horizon, restrict to the implied window. Print NOT APPLICABLE where a variation is undefined (a stop variation on a stop-less base), never skip silently.
4. For silence attribution, drop exactly one gate class as its own registered stream and count what appears, object by object. Then read the surviving gate's written demand against the fields its feed actually carries: a filter whose required field is absent from its feed fails closed by construction, and that is a defect candidate to cure or re-feed, not a strategy property to report.
5. If a transfer fails in threshold form, test its continuous form once, under a prediction stated in advance and on common trades, before closing the transfer. If both forms agree, the structure is descriptive only.
6. Print every counterweight at the same size as the headline: the family whose stop works, the horizon that is load-bearing, the prediction's misses.

**Worked example (ledger entry 15, the fail-closed filter).** All 49 sleeves of one family produced zero entries at base. Dropping only the alternative-data filter set as a separate stream revealed live entries in 14 sleeves (1 to 5 entries each) on the logic underneath. The surviving filter's demand explained the silence exactly: it requires an insider-buy field of 2 or more on the fire, a field no untagged fire carries, so on this feed the gate cannot pass by construction. The rest of the family was named, not lumped: 24 sleeves refuse faithfully because their universe is outside the cached universe (a faithful refusal by their own band rule), and 11 stay gated by their computable filters on the window. The registered forward check separates the two surviving hypotheses: shadow the dropped variants against the real fire feed, which carries the tags; if the tagged feed fires at a similar rate, the live silence is a tagging problem, not a logic problem.

**Second worked example (ledger entries 11 and 18, the stop, the failed prediction, and the continuous form).** Across 57 sleeves on 37 distinct entry streams, dropping or doubling one family's registered 15 percent stop adds paired per-trade deltas of plus 1,212.8 and plus 1,423.0 basis points (n 28), plus 1,029.1 and plus 1,021.0 (n 34), plus 676.4 and plus 654.1 (n 80), paired t between 1.12 and 1.91. The counterweights printed beside it: one family's stop works as a pair with its partial target (dropping it costs minus 213.3, n 56, t minus 2.2), one family's horizon extension is its positive (plus 497.7, n 58, t 2.28) while its stop drop is mildly negative, and one family's registered 21-session horizon is load-bearing (extension minus 604.3, n 280, t minus 4.06). In the same run, the prior lane's phase decomposition had implied a prediction (early-only exits help early-shaped families and hurt late-shaped ones). Stated in advance and tested as a rule on a different population, it confirmed in 1 of 4 classified families, on 5 paired trades that cannot carry a confirmation, and missed in the opposite direction on the deepest family (pooled minus 176.6 basis points, n 1,231, t minus 2.18). The continuous form of the same transfer (fitted remaining-alpha scale-outs) was then run once on the identical population: in the deepest family it reproduced the crude failure almost exactly (minus 177.0, t minus 2.19; the fitted schedule exits 95.5 percent of every position at the first close, so it is the crude rule wearing a curve), and in the two arrival families plain horizon extension dominated it (plus 168.0 at t 3.64 and plus 167.2 at t 2.22, against the decay arm's plus 52.8 at t 1.47 and plus 14.6 at t 0.26). Two sleeve-level leads survived at their own strength (one stream at plus 491.0, t 4.62, n 32, capturing 94 percent of the early arm's gain while exiting over a mean of 7.0 legs). Verdict: phase means are descriptive in both forms; exit design starts from the arms that actually carried deltas.

**Failure mode.** A variation run unpaired, or against an unlabelled base, measures populations, not rules. A diagnostic drop that quietly becomes the production rule has skipped its forward check: the dropped variant is shadowed against the real feed first, always.

**Geology signature.** Location: replay streams produced by the estate's own harness; sleeve code read against fire records. Depth: the code layer (registered parameters, filter demands) read against replay streams. Soil: dozens of objects on their own distinct entry streams, bases labelled. Clusters: Vein 2 (the gate-and-filter vein) and Vein 3 (the exit-and-phase vein); the boundary between them is where this method's twin results sit.

## Method 6: Lawful rebuild at an honest count

**What it is.** Rebuild any inherited find lawfully, from point-in-time data, discarding the unlawful evidence the original rested on; charge it to the family trial count it actually belongs to; run the full admission exam at that count; and when exactly one exam leg fails, interrogate the leg before burying the find.

**Trigger.** Reach for it whenever an object arrives with a grade it did not earn on this estate's current evidence: an inherited research object, a claim carried from a pre-cure record, a near-miss one leg short of the bar. The observable sign is a verdict (pass or corpse) resting on evidence nobody would accept if it were produced today.

**Procedure.**
1. Rebuild the object under point-in-time discipline. Discard the unlawful evidence explicitly; name what was discarded.
2. Charge the rebuild to the family count (the count goes up by the rebuild, never resets to 1).
3. Run the full admission exam at that count: deflated Sharpe, SPA against both benchmarks, probability of backtest overfitting, t, at the family's registered bars.
4. If exactly one leg fails, price the leg's own fragility before deciding the member's fate: leave-one-out composition reads across the family at neighbouring counts show whether the failing statistic is a property of the member or of the matrix it sits in.
5. Register the disposition at its true strength: a near-miss at an honest count is a watch-grade object with a named forward check, not a pass and not a corpse.
6. For record sets declared content-less, census the identifier grammar and the producers' source before accepting the verdict: exact reconstruction routes hide in id suffixes and in unit-weight specifications, and each route earns its rows only by agreeing with an independent record. Refuse inexact routes in print (joins whose fields name a different object than the one claimed; inference from what was taken to what was refused).

## Method 8: Definition fidelity

**What it is.** A cell-defined object lives inside its founding definition. **Trigger.** Reach for it whenever two leads are about to be stacked, a lead is about to be graded on accrued rows, or a cell's membership rule has any slack in it (either/or clauses, mixed states counted to the convenient side). **Procedure.**

2. Cross the exact cell with the second axis and read all four quadrants, including the empty one. The empty quadrant may be the finding: an object that exists only inside one regime is one object, not two stackable ones.
3. Run the loosened-definition read and print it beside the exact read as a bound.

## The autopsy protocol

Every find gets an autopsy in the same pass that reports it. A reported find with no autopsy is an open defect, graded as such in the next audit, because a find whose producing method is not recorded is a one-off: it cannot be re-applied, its ground cannot be re-dug, and its claim cannot be checked against the default that would have missed it.

The autopsy has seven fields, plus a geology row. Write each field from the find's own source files, named inside the entry.

1. **THE FIND.** The object and its numbers at true strength, including the deflation statistics and the forward check it carries.
2. **THE FRAMEWORK.** The stance that organized the work (decomposition, lawful rebuild, provenance discipline), in one or two sentences.
3. **THE METHODS AND TECHNIQUES, IN ORDER.** The steps as actually run, in the order run, with the counts at each stage.
4. **THE MINDSET.** The belief that made the steps obvious once stated (a basket return is a bundle, not a fact; recency is a bias, not a sample).
5. **WHAT I DID DIFFERENTLY.** The default behaviour, taken from the record, that would have missed the find, and the specific deviation that produced it. This field is the autopsy's core: it converts a result into a transferable correction.
6. **THE REUSABLE RULE.** The one-sentence rule the find teaches, stated so it can be applied to ground it has never touched.
7. **WHERE IT APPLIES NEXT.** The single next application owed, named concretely enough to be graded later.

**Geology row.** LOCATION: the corpus surface the find was extracted from. DEPTH: the layer down it sat at (report layer, data layer, raw-row layer, code layer, or a manufactured layer). SOIL: the data substrate it was embedded in, with its span and count. CLUSTER: the vein it belongs to. TOOL: the technique that extracted it.

## Anti-patterns: the negative ledger

These practices all ran in the same hunt, consumed real compute or real attention, and produced no find. Each is named with what it produced instead, so the record separates what actually worked from what merely ran. Treat each as a standing warning, not as history.

- **Seat-score corroboration as evidence.** A panel of model seats read 23 study pairs as highly corroborated; recomputation confirmed only 15 of the 23, two seat favourites at 0.84 and 0.83 could not be confirmed at all, and an adversarial seat priced every lead at 0.01 to 0.05 while a connection seat chose NONE as the strongest object in 10 of 10 blocks. Proximity is not identity, and a matcher that cannot name the quantity it matched has matched nothing.

- **A flip operator as a historical-screen arm.** An operator made operable on a reconstructed near-miss set was carried by 212 candidates; exactly 2 reached a parent holding reconstructed events, and neither scored. The near-miss mass lives in the final sessions, where no forward window exists yet; the arm's only live form is forward accrual under shadow machinery.

The registered consequence was executed in the same pass: the mutation premise is demoted as a historical screen, and the multiplication moves to resolution throughput. A third campaign on stored history is not a plan; it is a refusal to read two registers.
- **Inexact reconstruction routes.** Three routes were examined and refused: a journal join whose field names the symbols a sizing rule blocked, not the instrument the intent carried (the join manufactures symbols the record never stated); snapshot replay from a store holding 8 files, 3 complete, recent cycles only (dead for the historical window); and inference from a day's fills (a fill proves what was taken, never what was refused). Yield: nothing, deliberately. The 344 rows the exact routes proved are worth more than a larger set graded on invented symbols.
- **Resting states.** Carrying an object as blocked, ungradable or impossible because its current route failed, and letting the label harden into a verdict. Every resting state in the hunt that was re-attacked with a revised hypothesis either dissolved (a 22-object impossible class, a 641-object blocked lump reduced to 65) or produced its true bound. A resting state is a routes-tried ledger with the next route unnamed; name it, or run it.

## Cold-start checklist

An LLM arriving cold runs this list. It assumes the skill's Edge Ledger discipline for any individual strategy; this list governs the hunt itself.

**Before the hunt.**
1. State the question and the kill condition in one breath each. Work that cannot state both does not run.
2. Register the falsifier for every candidate the hunt may produce: the forward count and the line, fixed before any result is read.
3. Fix the trial-count family each test will be charged to, and the deflation family for every census.
4. Identify the independent unit for clustering (night, session, entry date) before computing anything.
5. Inventory the estate's records one layer below the layer you have been reading: producers' code, working files, rotated logs, side captures, shared stores. List what exists before deciding anything is absent.
6. Read the vein map and the negative ledger. Plan the hunt as veins in yield order, and strike the anti-patterns from the plan by name.

**During the hunt.**
7. Apply the methods in ranked order to the unlooked area: decompose first, reroute every failure, regenerate one order up, condition at maximum span, run operators as diagnostics, rebuild inherited objects lawfully, manufacture missing dimensions, hold definitions verbatim, and read primary sources.
8. Print the denominator at every stage. Reduce every cell census to distinct objects before counting.
9. Print the loosened read beside the exact read, the unconditioned beside the conditioned, the pooled beside the clustered, the counterweights beside the headline.
10. Preserve every failure exactly (status, count, span) in a routes-tried ledger attached to the object it gates.
11. When a population is carried as blocked, bound the residual arithmetically before naming any external gate.

**After the hunt.**
12. Autopsy every find in the same pass (seven fields plus geology), assign its vein, and name the vein's next dig site the find creates or confirms.
13. Register every forward check in one of three states, in preference order: wired (a mechanism accrues it), dated (it fires on a named date or count), named-only (specified, no mechanism yet; an open row, not a resting state).
14. Record the negative yield with the same prominence as the finds: practices that ran and produced nothing enter the negative ledger with their cost.
15. Report status as GREEN, PARTIAL, BLOCKED or UNVERIFIED with the evidence pointer, and claim no coverage beyond the counted fraction printed in the same sentence.
